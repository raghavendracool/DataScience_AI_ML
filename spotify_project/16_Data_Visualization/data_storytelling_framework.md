# Data Storytelling Framework

## 1. Context

What is the business situation?

## 2. Question

What decision must be made?

## 3. Evidence

Which chart or metric answers the question?

## 4. Observation

What does the chart directly show?

## 5. Insight

Why does the observation matter?

## 6. Action Hypothesis

What should be tested?

## 7. Primary KPI

How will success be measured?

## 8. Guardrail KPIs

What must not become worse?

## 9. Limitation

What uncertainty remains?

## 10. Decision

```text
Test
Scale
Refine
Stop
```

## Example

```text
Context:
Power Streamers are highly engaged.

Evidence:
They have the highest listening and session frequency.

Observation:
They also show high advertisement skipping.

Insight:
The segment receives strong value but may experience advertisement friction.

Action:
Test Premium messaging focused on uninterrupted listening.

Primary KPI:
Premium conversion.

Guardrails:
Churn, complaints and retained Premium status.
```
