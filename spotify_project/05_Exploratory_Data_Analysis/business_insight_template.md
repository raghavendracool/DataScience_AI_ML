# Business Insight Template

Use this structure for every major EDA finding.

## Insight Title

A short business-friendly title.

## Statistical Observation

Describe what the chart or statistic shows.

Example:

```text
Daily listening minutes have a long right tail.
```

## Supporting Evidence

Record:

- Mean
- Median
- Percentiles
- Skewness
- Relevant chart
- Related variables

## Behavioral Interpretation

Explain what the pattern may mean about users.

## Business Impact

Connect the pattern to:

- Engagement
- Retention
- Churn
- Premium conversion
- Advertisement behavior
- Personalization
- Revenue

## Recommended Next Analysis

State what should be compared or tested next.

## Caution

Record alternative explanations and avoid claiming causation without evidence.
