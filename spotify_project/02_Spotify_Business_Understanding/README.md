---

# 1. Spotify Business Model

Spotify connects listeners, artists, advertisers and record labels.

Business Flow:

```text
Artists
   │
Music Catalog
   │
Spotify Platform
   │
Users
   ├── Free Users → Advertisement Revenue
   └── Premium Users → Subscription Revenue
```

Business Value:
- Personalized music
- Global streaming
- Continuous engagement

---

# 2. Free and Premium Users

| Free | Premium |
|------|----------|
| Ads | No Ads |
| Limited Skip | Unlimited Skip |
| Standard Audio | High Quality |
| No Offline | Offline Download |
| Free | Monthly Subscription |

Why upgrade?
- Better experience
- No interruptions
- Offline listening

# 5. User Retention

Retention means keeping users active over time.

Spotify improves retention using:
- Discover Weekly
- Release Radar
- AI DJ
- Wrapped

---

# 6. Customer Churn

Churn means users stop using Spotify or reduce activity.

Reasons:
- Poor recommendations
- Too many ads
- Better competitors
- Low engagement

Business impact:
- Lower revenue
- Lower retention

---

# 7. Premium Conversion

# 12. Why Segmentation is Needed

Without segmentation:
- Same recommendations for everyone
- Generic marketing
- Higher churn

With segmentation:
- Personalized recommendations
- Better campaigns
- Better retention
- Higher revenue

```text
Customer Data
      ↓
Machine Learning
      ↓
Customer Segments
      ↓
Personas
      ↓
Business Decisions
      ↓
Revenue Growth
```
