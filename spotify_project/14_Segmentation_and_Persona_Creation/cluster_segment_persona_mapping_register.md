## Rules

1. One active persona mapping per cluster-model version.
2. Persona name must be supported by profile evidence.
3. Needs, risks and opportunities must be written as hypotheses.
4. Business owner must review the action.
5. Persona mapping must be versioned.
6. Any major model retraining requires revalidation.
