## Rules

1. Every action must link to persona evidence.
2. Recommendations are hypotheses until tested.
3. Primary and guardrail KPIs are mandatory.
4. Impact and cost should be measured incrementally.
5. Stop conditions must be defined before launch.
6. Record final scale, refine or stop decision.
